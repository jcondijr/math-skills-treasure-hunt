About

Math Skills: Treasure Hunt was created by John and his son Jack. It helps kids practice times tables with one question at a time, fun reward reveals, and positive retry flow.

How it works

Pick a table, answer 10 questions, and unlock a reward for each correct answer. If the answer is wrong, JJ appears and the same question repeats until correct. Reward categories are based on Jack's interests.
